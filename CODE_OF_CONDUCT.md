# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, experience, identity, or opinions. This is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to servers, Git or CI/CD.
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Respect others' privacy: never post someone else's credentials, server details or personal data.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information (including secrets) of others without consent.
- Spam, or advertising unrelated to the project.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions). Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block users that violate it. To report a concern, use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment, or contact the maintainers through the contact option in the repository's profile. Reports are handled privately and fairly.
